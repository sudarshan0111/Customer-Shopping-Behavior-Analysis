# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional retail data to uncover insights into customer preferences, spending patterns, product performance, and subscription behavior.

The objective is to help businesses make data-driven decisions by identifying trends, customer segments, and key factors influencing purchasing behavior.

---

## Business Problem

A retail company wants to better understand customer shopping patterns to improve sales performance, customer engagement, retention, and marketing effectiveness.

This project answers questions related to:

- Customer spending behavior
- Product popularity
- Customer loyalty
- Subscription effectiveness
- Discount impact on sales
- Revenue contribution by customer segments

---

## Key Insights

- Loyal customers represented the largest customer segment.
- Clothing generated the highest revenue contribution.
- Female and male customers showed different purchasing patterns.
- Discount campaigns significantly influenced certain product categories.
- Express shipping customers showed higher average spending.
- Subscription customers contributed substantial recurring revenue.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Strengthen customer loyalty programs.
- Improve subscription benefits and promotions.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-value customer segments.
- Promote top-rated products in campaigns.
- Optimize discount strategies for profitability.
